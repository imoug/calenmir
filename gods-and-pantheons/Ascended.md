The **Ascended** are beings who have transcended their mortal forms through extreme spiritual, intellectual, or magical evolution. In [[Calenmir]], ascension is a rare and mythical process, often tied to the mastery of balance between the Void and Light (chaos and order), the **Weave of Fate**, or ancient, forgotten rituals. These entities exist on a higher plane of existence, possessing godlike knowledge and powers that allow them to observe, influence, or even reshape reality, though often bound by codes of non-interference or their own inscrutable motivations.

Ascended beings are not bound by the physical laws of Calenmir. They may appear as radiant figures, shadowy echoes, or formless voices in the wind, depending on their origin and alignment. Some seek to preserve the balance of the Weave, while others aim to unravel it, spread chaos, or simply exist in perfect isolation.

Some races worship the Ascended who appear to them as gods, or unknowingly as part of ancestor worship.

## What is Ascension?
In Calenmir, ascension is a rare, almost mythical process where beings, through extreme spiritual, intellectual, or magical evolution, transcend their mortal forms. This might involve:
- **Mastery of the balance between order and chaos**: Drawing power from the cosmic void (a central theme in Calenmir's lore) to achieve enlightenment or godhood.
- **The Weave of Fate**: Ascended beings could exist outside the "Weave," the metaphysical tapestry that governs reality in Calenmir, allowing them to observe or subtly influence events without being bound by its rules.
- **Sacrifice or Ritual**: Ascension might require a great personal sacrifice (e.g., abandoning one's mortal identity, memories, or emotions) or a ritual tied to ancient, forgotten magic.

## Society and Culture
Ascended beings do not form a traditional society. Instead, they exist as solitary or loosely connected entities, each pursuing their own inscrutable goals. Their "culture" is defined by their alignment and domain:
- **The Weavers**: Operate in harmony with the Weave, guiding mortals through dreams, omens, and cryptic messages. They value balance, order, and the preservation of cosmic harmony.
- **The Hollow Ones**: Exist to spread chaos and corruption, often seeking to unravel the Weave or consume mortal souls to fuel their power.
- **The Silent Watchers**: Observe from the Ether, rarely intervening unless absolutely necessary. They are detached but not malevolent, often acting as neutral arbiters of cosmic events.
- **The Forgotten**: Lost between realms, these ascended beings whisper half-truths and seek release from their eternal limbo. They may bargain with mortals for a chance to return to the material plane or achieve true transcendence.

## Physical Traits
Ascended beings no longer possess physical forms as mortals understand them. Their appearances are manifestations of their essence and alignment:
- **The Weavers**, **The Architects**: Radiant, luminous figures often surrounded by threads of golden light, symbolizing their connection to the Weave.
- **The Hollow Ones**: Shadowy, distorted forms with hollow eyes, their presence warping reality around them.
- **The Silent Watchers**: Formless voices or floating, ever-watchful eyes, often invisible unless they choose to reveal themselves.
- **The Forgotten**: Flickering, translucent figures, like fading candles or echoes of their former selves.

## Abilities and Traits
Ascended beings possess a range of godlike abilities, though their powers are often limited by their alignment and the rules of their ascension:
- **Mastery of the Weave**: The ability to observe or subtly influence the threads of fate, guiding or testing mortals.
- **Void Manipulation**: Corrupted ascended beings can warp reality and slow time.
- **Etheric Presence**: The ability to exist in multiple planes simultaneously, often appearing as voices, visions, or omens.
- **Non-Interference**: Many ascended beings are bound by a code that prevents them from acting directly in the mortal realm, believing only mortals can truly affect the mortal realm, forcing them to work through avatars, artifacts, or chosen mortals.
- **Immortality**: Ascended beings do not age or die by conventional means, though they can be "destroyed" or banished under extreme circumstances.

## Notable Ascended Beings
- **The First Weaver**: The first being to ascend through mastery of the Weave, now a symbol of balance and cosmic harmony.
- **The Hollow King**: A former Guardian who fell to the Void's corruption, now seeking to unravel the Weave and consume all of Calenmir.
- **The Watcher in the Ether**: A Silent Watcher who has observed Calenmir since the dawn of time, intervening only to prevent cosmic collapse.
- **The Forgotten Poet**: A once-great scholar who ascended but became lost between realms, now whispering prophecies and half-truths to those who listen.

## Roleplaying an Ascended Being
If you wish to roleplay an ascended being in Calenmir, consider the following:
- **Speech**: Ascended beings often speak in riddles, metaphors, or cryptic phrases. Their voices may echo or resonate with unnatural power.
- **Motivations**: What is your goal? Are you preserving balance, spreading chaos, or seeking release? How do you view mortals: tools, pawns, or potential equals?
- **Limitations**: Remember that most ascended beings cannot act directly in the mortal realm. How do you work around this? Do you use avatars, artifacts, or chosen mortals?
- **Appearance**: How do you manifest to mortals? Are you a radiant figure, a shadowy echo, or a formless voice?

## Adventure Hooks
- **The Trial of the Weave**: The players are chosen to undergo a series of trials set by the Weavers to prove their worthiness to receive a fragment of ascended knowledge.
- **The Shattered Avatar**: A fragment of an ascended being's consciousness is trapped in a mortal vessel. The players must decide whether to free it, destroy it, or bargain with it.
- **The Architect's Gambit**: An ascended being is subtly manipulating the players' actions to fulfill a prophecy. Do they resist or comply?
- **The Withdrawn's Slumber**: A region of Calenmir is frozen in time due to the presence of a Withdrawn being. The players must enter this pocket of reality to retrieve an artifact or rescue someone trapped there.
- **The Hollow Crown**: A Corrupted ascended being seeks to claim a powerful artifact that could unravel the Weave. The players must stop them before it's too late.
