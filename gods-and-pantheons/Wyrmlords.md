**Type:** Pantheon  
**Alternative names:**   
_The Wyrm Lords_  
**Pantheon of:** dragonkin in [[Ubune|the south]]  

A pantheon of ancient dragons worshipped as gods primarily by dragonkin in [[Ubune|the south]].

## Roster
- [[Aetherion]], the Shining Queen; fire and life. Representing red dragons. (life & light domains)
- [[Terrath]], the Earthshaker; mountains, earthquakes and volcanoes. Representing silver dragons. (death, nature & tempest domains)
- [[Hydross]], the Deepwater; water and the depths. Representing blue dragons. (life, nature & tempest domains)
- [[Veridian]], the Lifebloom; growth, renewal and nature. Representing green dragons. (life, nature & peace domains)
- [[Umbrax]], the Shadow Weaver; shadow and hidden places. Representing black dragons. (knowledge, nature & twilight domains)
- [[Cryos]], the Frostheart; ice and winter. Representing white dragons. (death & tempest domains)
- [[Aurum]], the Forgeborn; smiths, artisans and all who work with metal. Representing gold dragons. (forge & order domains)
- [[Kairos]], the Timeless; time and fate. Representing bronze dragons. (arcana & trickery domains)

## The Wyrm Lords
- **Aetherion, the Shining Queen**: The eldest and most powerful of the Wyrm Lords, Aetherion commands the celestial fires and the storms of the heavens. She is the embodiment of raw draconic power and the ultimate authority. Her scales shimmer like a thousand stars, and her breath is pure lightning.
- **Terrath, the Earthshaker**: A colossal dragon whose scales are the color of granite and obsidian, Terrath is the master of earthquakes, volcanoes, and the very bones of the earth. He sleeps beneath the mountains, and his restless stirrings cause tremors across the land.
- **Hydross, the Deepwater Leviathan**: A serpentine dragon of immense size, Hydross rules the oceans and the abyssal depths. His scales are an iridescent blue-green, and his gaze can command the tides and summon monstrous sea creatures. He is a creature of mystery and ancient power.
- **Veridian, the Lifebloom Dragon**: Unlike the other Wyrm Lords, Veridian is a force of growth and renewal. His scales are a vibrant emerald green, and his breath can heal and restore. He is the guardian of forests, jungles, and all things that grow.
- **Umbrax, the Shadow Weaver**: A cunning and enigmatic dragon, Umbrax commands the shadows and the hidden places. His scales are a deep, almost black purple, and he can manipulate darkness to conceal himself and his followers. He is a master of secrets and whispers.
- **Cryos, the Frostheart**: A dragon of ice and winter, Cryos dwells in the highest mountains, where the air is thin and the cold bites deep. His scales are a glacial white, and his breath can freeze anything it touches. He is a creature of cold logic and unyielding will.
- **Aurum, the Gilded Queen**: Unlike the other Wyrm Lords, Aurum is not a dragon in the traditional sense. She is a being of pure metallic energy, her form shifting and shimmering like liquid gold. She is the patron of smiths, artisans, and all who work with metal.
- **Kairos, the Timeless Wyrm**: An ancient and enigmatic dragon, Kairos exists outside the normal flow of time. His scales are a swirling bronze, and he is said to possess knowledge of the past, present, and future. He speaks in riddles and prophecies.

## Servants
- **Drakonids**: Reptilian humanoids, often serving as warriors, priests, or scribes in the service of the Wyrm Lords.
- **Wyverns**: Smaller, more agile draconic creatures, often used as mounts or messengers.
- **Elementals**: Beings of pure elemental energy, often bound to the service of the Wyrm Lords.
- **Dragonborn**: Mortals with draconic blood, often possessing innate magical abilities or draconic traits.
