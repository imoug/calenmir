**Type:** Pantheon  
**Alternative names:**  

The nature gods of [[Calenmir]] formed after the defeat of the primal ones from the body of the fallen Guardian [[Yrnia]], the guardian of all nature. Because of this the Nairene have a strong connection to the natural world along with their own personal portfolios. They were tasked with watching over the world by the remaining Guardians and helped them rebuild Calenmir after the ravages of the Dawn War. For a long time the Nairene lived in the virgin world of Calenmir amongst the first born races of elves, dwarves and gnomes.
 
---
 
It’s said that something wild lurks in the heart of every soul, a space that thrills to the sound of geese calling at night, to the whispering wind through the pines, to the unexpected red of mistletoe on an oak—and it is in this space that the Nairene dwell. Though they were born of the fallen Guardian [[Yrnia]] and not of brook and stream, their might is heightened by the strength of the oak and the beauty of the woodlands and open moor, and when the first forester dared put a name to the face seen in the bole of a tree or the voice babbling in a brook, it was the Nairene who answered.

The Nairene are as often served by druids as by clerics, for they are closely aligned with the forces of nature that druids revere.
 
The Nairene represent life, wonder and nature.

---
#### **The Mark of the Nairene:**

- Those **blessed by the Nairene** bear the **Mark of the Wild**—a **spiral sigil** that **glows faintly** in moonlight.
- The mark **grants protection** from the wild’s dangers, but also **binds the bearer** to the Nairene’s will.
- Betraying this trust **invokes the wrath of the land itself**.
### **The Nairene’s Gifts and Curses**

The Nairene **do not trade**, but they **reward those who honor them** and **punish those who do not**.

#### **1. Blessings of the Wild:**

- **The Green Touch:** A **blessing of growth**, making **crops flourish** and **wounds heal**.
- **The Beast’s Tongue:** The ability to **speak with animals**, granted to those who **protect the wilds**.
- **The Unseen Path:** The **ability to walk unnoticed** through the forest, even in plain sight.

#### **2. Curses of the Land:**

- **The Withering:** Crops **rot overnight**, and **livestock sicken** without cause.
- **The Lost Way:** Travelers **walk in circles** for days, seeing **phantom paths** that lead nowhere.
- **The Hungry Earth:** The **ground swallows the greedy**, leaving only **a pair of boots** behind.

#### **Abilities and Traits

#### **1. The Mistwalk:**

- The Nairene can **step into the mist** and **reappear elsewhere**, moving **as though the fog itself carries them**.
- Some say they **do not walk, but ride the wind**, and others claim they **are the mist given form**.

#### **2. The Song of the Veil:**

- Their **voices can weave illusions**, **calm beasts**, or **lull enemies into forgetfulness**.
- A Nairene’s **song can make a pursuer** **forget why they were chasing**, or **make a lost traveler** **find their way home**.

#### **3. The Gifts of the Old Ones:**

- The Nairene **craft items of strange power**:
    - **Mistcloaks:** Garments that **blend into the fog**, making the wearer **nearly invisible**.
    - **Whisperstones:** Stones that **carry messages** when thrown into a fire.
    - **Dreamroot:** A herb that **grants visions**—or **nightmares**, depending on the user’s heart.

#### **4. The Mark of the Veiled:**

- Those who **earn the Nairene’s trust** (a rare honor) are **marked with a sigil**—a **spiral rune** that **glows faintly in the dark**.
- The mark **grants protection** from the Nairene’s enemies, but also **binds the bearer to secrecy**. Betraying their trust **brings a curse**—**madness, misfortune, or worse**.
### **The Nairene’s Realms in Calenmir**

The Nairene **do not build cities**, but their **presence is felt** in **sacred places** across Calenmir.

#### **1. The Veilwood (Ceunan):**

- A **forest where the trees whisper**, and the **Nairene walk as shadows**.
- The **druids of Ceunan** **tend it with reverence**, and **outsiders who enter** often **lose their way**.

#### **2. The Mistveil Glades (Stormlands/Dales Border):**

- A **valley of perpetual twilight**, where the **Nairene hold court** with those they **deem worthy**.
- **Rangers and scouts** leave **offerings of food and song**, and **receive dreams of guidance** in return.

#### **3. The Stonecircle (Stormlands):**

- A **ring of standing stones**, where the **Nairene and the Stormlands warriors** **meet in secret**.
- It is said that **those who stand in the center** at dawn **hear the Divine Song**.

#### **4. The Whispering Caverns (Ironcliffs/Gumgan Border):**

- A **cave system** where the **walls hum with ancient runes**, and the **Nairene speak in echoes**.
- **Dwarven miners** **avoid it**, claiming it is **haunted by spirits** who **steal their tools and leave gold that turns to dust**.

#### **5. The Floating Isles (Gladed League Border):**

- **Islands of mist and wood**, drifting **above the forests** where the **Nairene watch the elves**.
- The **Gladed League** has **tried to claim them**, but their **scouts return mad or lost**.
