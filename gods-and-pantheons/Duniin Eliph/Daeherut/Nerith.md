Nerith, the [[Daeherut|Daeheran]] Goddess of Sheltering Illusion, Disguise and Theatre, opposition to the [[Nairene]] [[Woldros]]. Nerith is the illusion that shelters: the glamour over the refugee column, the mask at the theatre, the face the hunted wear to come home. She is Woldros' shadow, for the light that shows all things truly is cruel to those who cannot survive being seen.

## Depictions
A figure in stage-black holding a mirrored mask slightly away from the face, so the viewer is always uncertain which is the person.

## Holy Symbol
The holy symbol of Nerith, _A mirrored mask, smiling_, is worn openly by the clergy and marks the doors of the temples. Like all Daeheran symbols it is meant to be read alongside its Nairene counterpart, for each pair of faces makes one truth.

## Worship
Nerith's disguises shelter the hunted, and her clergy are called on the way others call on locksmiths: quietly, at night, for a good reason. Her theatres are the Daeherut's public face, and the masked plays of the Gods' Days, where lords may be mocked with impunity, are licensed in her name.

## Clergy
Clerics of Nerith keep the illusion-shrines: refuges hidden behind glamours, the hidden rooms of the persecuted, and the theatre itself. Their great rule is the Curtain: every illusion they cast must be formally ended, and an illusion never dropped is held to become a lie of Xarion's making, with all that implies.

## Practices and Rituals
The Masking of a column or a refuge; the Curtain rite, formally ending every glamour; the Gods' Days masked plays.

## Temples
Temples of Nerith are theatres, and no one who has never performed in one may say where the shrine actually is.

## Artifacts
The Sheltering Mask, a glamour that holds while the wearer protects another; the Falling Curtain, which cleanly ends any enchantment cast in the same rite.
