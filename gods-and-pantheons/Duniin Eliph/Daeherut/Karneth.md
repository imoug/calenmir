Karneth, the [[Daeherut|Daeheran]] God of Predators, the Clean Kill and the Cull, opposition to the [[Nairene]] [[Phutune]]. Karneth is the predator's half of nature: the clean kill, the culling of the old and the sick so the herd is strong, the mercy that the wild shows the suffering by ending them. He teaches that the hunter is part of the world, not its enemy, and owes the hunted more than the hunted owes him.

## Depictions
A grey-mantled huntsman with a wolf at heel, bow always unstrung until the moment it is needed, whose face is never shown twice alike.

## Holy Symbol
The holy symbol of Karneth, _A wolf head with closed eyes_, is worn openly by the clergy and marks the doors of the temples. Like all Daeheran symbols it is meant to be read alongside its Nairene counterpart, for each pair of faces makes one truth.

## Worship
Every ethical hunter in Calenmir says Karneth's word before the shot, "quick and clean", and thanks him after, cutting the first meat for the pack's share before their own. Wolf-packs follow his cult openly in the Stormlands, and the Dales' hunting clans keep his First Meat at table.

## Clergy
Clerics of Karneth lead the winter culls, track and end wounded beasts the hunters left, and prosecute poachers who kill for pleasure. In famine they are the ones who decide which herds must go, and their funerals for culled animals are not considered eccentric anymore.

## Practices and Rituals
The First Meat, the hunter's word before the shot; the Cull, led by his clergy each Embermonth; the Pack's Share, cut for the wild before any larder.

## Temples
Temples of Karneth are hunting lodges: a hall, a great hearth, and antlers of every cull season hung by the door. His holiest places are game trails, marked only by a worn stone.

## Artifacts
The Unstringed Bow, with which a shot cannot wound without killing; the Grey Mantle, which lets a cull be walked through a herd without scent or sound.
