Cryven, the [[Daeherut|Daeheran]] Goddess of Winter Stillness, Preservation and Suspended Grief, opposition to the [[Nairene]] [[Cailega]]. Cryven is winter's stillness rather than its cruelty: the seed's sleep in the frozen ground, the numbness that carries the grieving through the first days, and the preservation of what must be kept unchanged until it can be safely felt again.

**Type:** Deity  
**Demanse:** _[[The Frostmere]]_

## Depictions
A pale woman in grey-white, still as a frozen lake, holding a single seed in an ice case; her expression is not empty but waiting.

## Holy Symbol
The holy symbol of Cryven, _A snowflake etched onto a shard of ice_, is worn openly by the clergy and marks the doors of the temples. Like all Daeheran symbols it is meant to be read alongside its Nairene counterpart, for each pair of faces makes one truth.

## Worship
Cryven receives the grief that cannot yet be felt: mourners who cannot weep ask her to "hold it until I can", and her Frostfast rite, where grief is formally laid down until a chosen thaw-day, is the kindest custom Calenmir's north knows. The frost crystals of the Coldanvil are kept in her name for preservation.

## Clergy
Clerics of Cryven keep the cold-stores of temples and the ice-houses of northern holds, and tend the emotionally frozen with the patience of thaw. Their vows forbid them to force feeling on anyone: they only make sure spring comes, eventually, and are very hard to hurry.

## Practices and Rituals
The Frostfast, grief formally held until a chosen day; the Seed's Sleep, the blessing of anything laid down for winter; the Thaw, the equally formal taking-up of what was held.

## Temples
Temples of Cryven are ice-houses: vaulted cold cellars with a single seed-case at the centre, and candles lit at Stillnight and not again until Greenrise.

## Artifacts
The Held Seed, which keeps one thing safe and unchanged, but only while it is never looked at; the Thaw Lamp, which lights when its keeper is ready to feel again.
