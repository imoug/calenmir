Vessith, the [[Daeherut|Daeheran]] Goddess of Madness, Visions and the Sleepless, opposition to the [[Nairene]] [[Xoemis]]. Vessith is the mind's edge: the vision that comes with the breaking of sleep, the truth the sane agree not to see, the madness that is sometimes revelation. She is not the enemy of dreams but their far shore, and her charges are the sleepless, the haunted and the different-minded.

## Depictions
A wide-eyed figure in a night robe with a broken clock for a heart, speaking in three voices that always finish one another's sentences.

## Holy Symbol
The holy symbol of Vessith, _A broken clock whose hands still move_, is worn openly by the clergy and marks the doors of the temples. Like all Daeheran symbols it is meant to be read alongside its Nairene counterpart, for each pair of faces makes one truth.

## Worship
Vessith's Sleepless Houses, Calenmir's asylums, are the quiet pride of the Daeherut faiths: places the sleepless and the haunted are kept safe, not cured by force, and listened to. Portents are brought to her oracle-sleepers, and the mad are formally prohibited from being mocked, a law several nations actually enforce.

## Clergy
Clerics of Vessith keep the Sleepless Houses, tend the sleepless through their nights, and train the oracle-sleepers whose ravings are carefully written down. Their discipline is the hardest of the Balance: to listen without judging what is vision and what is illness, and to write it all down anyway.

## Practices and Rituals
The Nightsitting, a vigil kept with the sleepless; the Recording, where ravings and portents are written and archived; the Cracked Hour at Stillnight, when her Houses stay lit all night.

## Temples
Temples of Vessith are the Sleepless Houses: comfortable, softly lit, and always open, with their clocks deliberately set all to different times.

## Artifacts
The Cracked Hourglass, which shows the true hour for visions only; the Patient Key, which opens any Sleepless House to someone in need, and nothing else.
