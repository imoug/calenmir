Korvath, the [[Daeherut|Daeheran]] God of Wildfire, Honest Battle and the Clearing, opposition to the [[Nairene]] [[Geddal]]. Korvath is not slaughter but the honest violence of the world: the wildfire that clears the old growth, the storm that fells the rotten limb, the fist swung in the open. He is what makes room, and what he clears, Vereth returns.

**Type:** Deity

## Depictions
A scarred, red-bearded warrior of middle years whose armour is always dented and never clean, laughing in the way storms laugh.

## Holy Symbol
The holy symbol of Korvath, _A clawed hand wreathed in flame_, is worn openly by the clergy and marks the doors of the temples. Like all Daeheran symbols it is meant to be read alongside its Nairene counterpart, for each pair of faces makes one truth.

## Worship
The Stormlands' warriors swear by him before every border ride, and the "Red Oath", fought openly, first blow announced, no poison, is his. Backwoodsmen of the Dales and the Amberwood light controlled burns in his name each Frostwane, though they will tell you it is just good forestry.

## Clergy
Clerics of Korvath are battle-priests who fight at the front, carry no hidden weapons, and despise assassins as a blasphemy. They act as marshals of honest single combat, and a duel sanctified by one is considered settled before witnesses anywhere in Calenmir.

## Practices and Rituals
The First Blow, announced before any sanctioned violence; the Emberfast, a burn cleared and watched with prayer until the last ember dies; the Storm's End, where warriors who have fought must sit together afterwards.

## Temples
Temples of Korvath are fire-circles of blackened standing stones with the marks of old fires still in them; none has ever been built with a roof.

## Artifacts
The Emberwood Shield, burn-proof and said to grow warm when a hidden foe draws near; the Red Horn, whose blast makes an announced challenge binding in law as well as faith.
