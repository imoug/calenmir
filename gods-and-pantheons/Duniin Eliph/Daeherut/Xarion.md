Xarion, the [[Daeherut|Daeheran]] God of Necessary Lies, Secrets and Satire, opposition to the [[Nairene]] [[Reraura]]. Xarion is the necessary lie: the alias that saves the hunted, the flattery that prevents a war, the satire that pricks what power would not otherwise feel. He is Reraura's shadow, not her enemy, for words that only ever tell the truth are a cage.

## Depictions
A slight figure in motley whose mask is on the back of the head, so you never know which face is speaking.

## Holy Symbol
The holy symbol of Xarion, _A forked tongue curled into a ring_, is worn openly by the clergy and marks the doors of the temples. Like all Daeheran symbols it is meant to be read alongside its Nairene counterpart, for each pair of faces makes one truth.

## Worship
Xarion receives the confessions Reraura cannot: the alias of a witness in hiding, the false paternity that keeps a child safe, the smuggler's manifest. His one dreaded aspect is as keeper of the ledger of broken oaths: liars who harm the innocent pray to him for mercy, and receive silence.

## Clergy
Clerics of Xarion serve as confessors to spies, smugglers and the hunted, and their seal is honoured even by courts that despise them. They are also Calenmir's satirists, licensed insulters of the powerful whose japes cannot be prosecuted during the Gods' Days.

## Practices and Rituals
The Substitution, a life-saving alias formally given into Xarion's keeping; the Fools' Hour, the annual day when satires may be spoken to any lord; the Reckoning of Words, a private rite by which an oathbreaker asks whether their broken word may be mended.

## Temples
Temples of Xarion are theatres in all but name: one stage, no windows, and doors that look like walls. Congregants enter masked.

## Artifacts
The Forked Quill, which writes two truths, one visible, one hidden; the Motley Ring, which grants the wearer one unchallenged lie per year.
