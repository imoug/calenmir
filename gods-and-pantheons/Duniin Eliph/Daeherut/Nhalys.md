Nhalys, the [[Daeherut|Daeheran]] Goddess of Night, Rest and the Gentle Dark, opposition to the [[Nairene]] [[Neulla]]. Not the void that hungers, but the night that restores: Nhalys is the dark that closes over each day like a blanket drawn up, the privacy of shadow, and the keeper of honest sleep. Her sister's light makes things grow, but nothing grows that never rests.

## Depictions
A tall, veiled woman whose face no two viewers agree on, garbed in grey-blues and black shot with silver; her hands are soft and always empty.

## Holy Symbol
The holy symbol of Nhalys, _A black sun eclipsed by a crescent moon_, is worn openly by the clergy and marks the doors of the temples. Like all Daeheran symbols it is meant to be read alongside its Nairene counterpart, for each pair of faces makes one truth.

## Worship
Nhalys is worshipped nightly in the simplest of ways: covers drawn, the day released. Whispering the day's grievances to the dark before sleep is her household rite, for the dark keeps what it is given. She is the patron of hospice night-watches and of all who work while others sleep.

## Clergy
Clerics of Nhalys keep the night watches: at hospices, at cradles, at the beds of the dying. They are trusted with secrets the way Reraura's clergy are trusted with words, and a confession made to a priestess of Nhalys is considered already half-forgiven. They hold that insomnia is a spiritual debt and treat it accordingly.

## Practices and Rituals
The Drawing of the Veil at dusk, a moment of silence before the first lamp is lit; the Night-Claims, where a community's secrets are spoken once to the dark and never again; waking vigils for the sleepless and the grieving.

## Temples
Temples of Nhalys are blind-windowed, cool, quiet halls entered by one door only. No Nhalys temple has ever had windows facing another building.

## Artifacts
The Mantle of Hours, a veil said to hold one true night's rest for the person who most needs it; the Sleepless Bell, which rings softly to mark a debt of rest owed.
