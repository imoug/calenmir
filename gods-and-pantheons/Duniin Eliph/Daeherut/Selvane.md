Selvane, the [[Daeherut|Daeheran]] Goddess of Rest, the Sabbath and the Settling of Magic, opposition to the [[Nairene]] [[Manera]]. Selvane is the rest between the notes: the sabbath, the pause between spells, the still water that lets the Weave settle. She is not the enemy of magic but its convalescence, for a Weave that is never at rest frays, and a mage who never rests breaks.

## Depictions
A robed figure seated, palms open and empty, with a single banked ember at her feet: not dead, only resting.

## Holy Symbol
The holy symbol of Selvane, _A withered hand releasing an ember_, is worn openly by the clergy and marks the doors of the temples. Like all Daeheran symbols it is meant to be read alongside its Nairene counterpart, for each pair of faces makes one truth.

## Worship
Selvane keeps the Quiet Hour: one hour in the week when mages of the Duniin Eliph traditions work no magic at all, and Tivelli clockmakers famously "let a gear rest" in her honour. Apprentices who skip it are set to scrub floors, and masters who skip it are quietly retired.

## Clergy
Clerics of Selvane tend burnt-out mages the way Vereth's tend wounds, and their hospices are where arcanists go to remember how to be people. They also serve as arbiters of rest in overworked guilds and armies, and their word that a company is spent carries legal force in several nations.

## Practices and Rituals
The Quiet Hour, kept weekly; the Banked Fire, where an enchanter formally lays down a long project for a season; the Settling, a still pool gazed into before any great working.

## Temples
Temples of Selvane are quiet rooms with almost nothing in them: no altar images, one candle, and comfortable seats. The greatest of them is a disused Nairene shrine given to her, so that the site too could rest.

## Artifacts
The Unwound Key, which lays dormant any enchantment for a day, harmlessly; the Banked Ember, which never lights for a person who does not need to be up.
