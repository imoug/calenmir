Skaelith, the [[Daeherut|Daeheran]] God of Critique, Demolition and Art from Broken Things, opposition to the [[Nairene]] [[Certhys]]. Skaelith is art's other face: the demolition that precedes the building, the satire that keeps beauty honest, the mosaic made of works that were shattered on purpose. He is Certhys' rival and her whetstone both, and every artist who fears him owes him.

## Depictions
A figure in a workman's apron holding a hammer in one hand and a finished mosaic in the other, whose own face is deliberately unfinished.

## Holy Symbol
The holy symbol of Skaelith, _A canvas slashed through by a clean single line_, is worn openly by the clergy and marks the doors of the temples. Like all Daeheran symbols it is meant to be read alongside its Nairene counterpart, for each pair of faces makes one truth.

## Worship
Skaelith receives the artist's self-judgment: the smashed first draft, the burned manuscript, the fourth wall taken down and rebuilt. His Reputations rite, where an artist formally invites the harshest critique of their peers, is feared and respected in equal measure, and no great gallery in Calenmir hangs a work that has not passed one.

## Clergy
Clerics of Skaelith are the licensed critics, the demolition-keepers of the art world, and, less famously, the salvage-carpenters who take apart ruin and building alike for rebuilding. They consecrate controlled demolitions, and Tivelli hires them constantly.

## Practices and Rituals
The Slashing, where a flawed work is destroyed by its maker before witnesses; the Reputations rite of formal critique; the Remaking, where the shards of a Slashed work become a new one.

## Temples
Temples of Skaelith are half gallery, half demolition ground: beautiful rooms full of rubble that is being sorted.

## Artifacts
The Honest Chisel, which cuts only what is flawed; the Slashed Canvas, upon which whatever is destroyed reforms into new art.
