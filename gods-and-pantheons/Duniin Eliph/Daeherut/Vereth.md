Vereth, the [[Daeherut|Daeheran]] Goddess of Decay, Compost and the Return, opposition to the [[Nairene]] [[Iaras]]. Not corruption for its own sake, but the world's recycler: Vereth is the slow, patient power that takes what is finished and returns it to what can be used again. Every compost heap, every returning leaf, every wound that must be cleaned before it can close is hers.

**Type:** Deity

## Depictions
An elderly herbalist with moss at her cuffs, one hand offering a leaf and the other turning it to mulch; her smile is kind, and her gardens are always half-fallow.

## Holy Symbol
The holy symbol of Vereth, _A skull entwined with harmless vines that flower_, is worn openly by the clergy and marks the doors of the temples. Like all Daeheran symbols it is meant to be read alongside its Nairene counterpart, for each pair of faces makes one truth.

## Worship
Farmers bless the first cutting of the compost heap in Seedfall in Vereth's name, and the sick pray to her for a "clean rot", a healing that goes all the way down. Gravediggers, tanners and dyers honour her before they begin, because theirs are her trades.

## Clergy
Clerics of Vereth are the plague-doctors and quarantine-keepers of the Duniin Eliph faiths, respected and kept at arm's length in equal measure. They brew antidotes as readily as poisons, and are the only clergy trusted by both sides of a plague line. Their great vow is to never let a wound fester out of squeamishness.

## Practices and Rituals
The Turning of the Heap each Seedfall; the Cleansing, where infected goods are ritually rotted and returned; the Quiet Gate, the last anointing of bodies too ruined for an open funeral.

## Temples
Temples of Vereth are walled gardens with open compost hearts at the centre, and the best of them grow healing herbs nowhere else in Calenmir can match.

## Artifacts
The Garden Fork of Vereth, a simple iron fork said to speed any compost to usefulness; the Vial of Last Resort, which can tell poison from cure by the colour of its smoke.
