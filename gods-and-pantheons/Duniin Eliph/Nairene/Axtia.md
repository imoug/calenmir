Axtia, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] Goddess of Life and Death in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Mordhal]]. Axtia embodies the delicate balance between life and death. She is depicted as a figure with one half veiled in shadows and the other bathed in light. Followers of Axtia revere the cycle of existence and believe that death is a natural transition to a higher plane of existence.

## Depictions
A tall and gaunt human woman in white tunic and black or grey robes. She is portrayed as either blind or blindfolded. She carries a club with the power of life and death and a staff with a black orb atop it encasing a white star.

She has grey and gold eyes, long streaming black hair and a pale complexion.

## Holy Symbol
Axtia's holy symbol, a conjoined black and white star with ten points, holds profound significance in her domain of life and death. The black half of the star represents the mystery and shadows of death, while the white half signifies the purity and light of life. The ten points symbolize the cyclical nature of existence, reflecting the idea that life and death are intertwined and part of a greater cosmic rhythm. This symbol serves as a reminder of Axtia's role as the deity who oversees the delicate balance between the two realms, guiding her followers to embrace the natural transitions that occur in the world of Calenmir.

## Further Lore
Axtia, the Goddess of Life and Death, a mysterious figure of the afterlife.
### Practices and Rituals
- Ancestral Veneration: Axtia's followers honor their ancestors, believing that they continue to exist in the afterlife. Regular rituals are performed to honor them, offering prayers and sacrifices to ensure their well-being there.
- Death Rites: Elaborate funeral rites are conducted to guide the souls of the departed into the afterlife.
- Dream Interpretation: Dreams are seen as a way to communicate with the spirit world and gain insight into the future.
- Necromancy and Spirit Communication: Some practitioners of Axtia's magic specialize in necromancy and spirit summoning, seeking guidance from the departed.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Cycle of Life and Death: Life and death are interconnected, and death is not the end, but a transition to another realm.
- Respect for the Dead: The dead are treated with reverence, and their spirits are honored.
- The Power of the Underworld: Axtia's followers recognize the power and mystery of the underworld, and they seek to understand its secrets.
- The Balance of Life and Death: Both are essential to the cosmic order.
- The Inevitability of Fate: Fate is an inescapable force, and everyone must eventually face death. Axtia's followers are encouraged to live with purpose and meaning, and to prepare for their eventual passing.
### Festivals
- The Festival of the Crossing: Marks the transition between life and death, with rituals to honor the souls of the departed.
- The Festival of Renewal: Celebrates the cycle of life and death, with rituals to honor the power of rebirth.
- The Festival of the Veiled One: A solemn festival held in honor of Axtia's mysterious and often veiled nature.
### Clerics of Axtia
Clerics of Axtia often serve as guides for the dying and protectors of the dead. They are skilled in healing, necromancy, and divination.
### Temples of Axtia
Temples of Axtia are often located in secluded, often underground, places. They are typically dark and mysterious, with intricate carvings and symbols representing death and rebirth.
### Artifacts of Axtia
- The Scythe of Fate: A powerful weapon that can sever the thread of life.
- The Book of the Dead: A legendary tome containing knowledge of the afterlife and the secrets of death.
- The Cloak of Shadows: A magical cloak that allows the wearer to travel between the worlds of the living and the dead.

## Relationships
- [[Manera]]: A complex relationship of balance. While Manera brings life into the world, Axtia ensures that the cycle continues. They share a deep respect for each other's power and ultimately recognize the importance of their partnership in maintaining cosmic order, though their differing perspectives can sometimes lead to tension.
- [[Cailega]]: A tense alliance of opposites. Winter, associated with Cailega, is a time of dormancy and death, but it also prepares the ground for renewal. They often work together, albeit cautiously, though Cailega's capricious nature can clash with Axtia's more measured approach.
- [[Certhys]]: A respectful understanding. Certhys brings life and beauty into the world; Axtia oversees the transition from life to death. They maintain a respectful distance, recognizing the importance of each other's roles.
- [[Dhorer]]: A respectful understanding of creation and destruction. Dhorer's creations, however durable, eventually succumb to time and decay; Axtia ensures the cycle continues.
- [[Ephion]]: A respectful understanding of life and death. Ephion brings new life into the world; Axtia oversees the transition from life to death.
- [[Geddal]]: A respectful understanding. Geddal embodies the life force and the will to survive; his warriors, however strong, eventually succumb to death, and Axtia ensures the cycle continues.
