Certhys, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] Goddess of Travel and Commerce in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Skaelith]]. Certhys is a resourceful deity, watching over travelers and merchants. Her followers seek her blessings for safe travels and profitable endeavors.

## Depictions
She is depicted with a satchel and a map, guiding those on journeys both physical and metaphorical. She is often portrayed with shining yellow eyes and short blond hair.

Portrayed as a disorganized and haphazard half elf woman with ill fitting clothes, sturdy boots, backpack and always with paint on her person and clothes. She is shown with great whimsy and is known as a thoughtful and fierce negotiator.

## Holy Symbol
Certhys' holy symbols, either a laden wagon or a wagon wheel, perfectly embody her roles as the goddess of travel and commerce.
- The laden wagon symbolizes the journeys and connections formed through trade and exploration. It represents the exchange of goods, ideas, and cultures that Certhys oversees, fostering understanding and unity among diverse communities.
- The wagon wheel symbolizes the progression of travel and the interconnectedness of different places. It signifies the constant movement and flow of people and goods, which Certhys' followers navigate with her guidance.
- Both symbols serve as a reminder of Certhys' importance in facilitating communication, trade, and the bonds that form between distant lands in the world of Calenmir.

## Worship
A pinch of salt to Certhys is offered before a voyage.

## Further Lore
Certhys, the Wandering Goddess of Arts and Commerce.
### Practices and Rituals
- Art and Craft Workshops: Workshops are held to teach various artistic skills, from painting and sculpture to music and storytelling.
- Trade Caravans: Caravans are blessed before setting off on their journeys, seeking the protection of Certhys, and merchants often perform rituals to ensure safe and prosperous journeys.
- Pilgrimages: Many followers embark on pilgrimages to sacred sites associated with Certhys, seeking inspiration and enlightenment.
- Creative Expression: Certhys' followers are encouraged to express themselves through art, music, writing, and other creative pursuits.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Power of Creativity: Creativity is a divine gift, and followers strive to express themselves through art, music, and other creative pursuits.
- The Joy of Exploration: The spirit of adventure and exploration is valued, and followers are encouraged to seek new experiences and knowledge.
- The Importance of Trade and Commerce: Trade is seen as a way to connect people and cultures, and to promote prosperity and understanding.
- The Divine in the Mundane: The divine can be found in the everyday, and even the simplest acts of creation can be acts of worship.
- The Pursuit of Knowledge: Knowledge is seen as a valuable commodity, and followers are encouraged to seek wisdom and understanding.
### Festivals
- The Festival of Spring: A grand celebration marking the arrival of spring, featuring music, dance, poetry, and feasting.
- The Festival of the Wandering Soul: A festival honoring Certhys' love of travel and exploration, with storytelling, music, and performances from various cultures.
- The Festival of the Market: A celebration of trade and commerce, featuring markets, fairs, and performances by street artists.
### Clerics of Certhys
Clerics of Certhys are often bards, minstrels, or merchants. They use their skills to inspire others, spread knowledge, and promote peace and prosperity.
### Temples of Certhys
Temples of Certhys are often located near crossroads or along trade routes. They are typically bustling places, filled with the sounds of music, laughter, and the clinking of coins.
### Artifacts of Certhys
- The Wand of Inspiration: A magical wand that can inspire creativity and spark innovation.
- The Boots of the Wandering Soul: A pair of boots that allow the wearer to travel effortlessly across vast distances.
- The Grimoire of the Infinite: A legendary tome containing knowledge of the arts, sciences, and the mysteries of the universe.

## Relationships
- [[Manera]]: A harmonious partnership of creativity and magic. Magic can inspire art, and art can inspire magic; both value knowledge, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, and often work together to inspire and uplift their followers.
- [[Axtia]]: A respectful understanding of life and death. Certhys brings life and beauty into the world; Axtia oversees the transition from life to death. They maintain a respectful distance.
- [[Cailega]]: A cautious alliance of opposites. Cailega's winter prepares the ground for the growth and renewal associated with Certhys' spring, though their contrasting natures sometimes lead to conflict.
- [[Dhorer]]: A harmonious partnership of creation and inspiration. Art can inspire craftsmanship, and skilled craftsmanship can bring artistic visions to life; both value creativity, innovation, and the pursuit of excellence.
- [[Ephion]]: A harmonious partnership of creativity and love. Art can inspire love, and love can inspire art; both encourage their followers to express themselves freely and celebrate the joys of life.
- [[Geddal]]: A cautious alliance of contrasting energies. Geddal's emphasis on action and physical prowess may clash with Certhys' more contemplative and creative nature, but both strength and creativity are necessary for survival and prosperity.
