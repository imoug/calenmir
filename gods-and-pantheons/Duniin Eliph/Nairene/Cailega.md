Cailega, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] Goddess of Winter, Winds, and Change in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Cryven]]. Cailega is a transformative deity, embodying the cold winds of winter and the inevitability of change. She is often depicted with frost-covered hair and swirling winds. Her followers embrace the cycles of nature and the transformative power of seasons.

## Depictions
A veiled old human woman with one eye. She wears dark ragged clothes and carries a large gnarled walking stick topped with a grey orb. She is often depicted with frost-covered hair and swirling winds.

## Holy Symbol
Cailega's holy symbol is a snowflake encircled by swirling winds. The snowflake represents the beauty and uniqueness of winter, while the swirling winds symbolize the dynamic and ever-changing nature of her domains. This symbol elegantly captures Cailega's influence over the transformative power of seasons, the cold embrace of winter, and the winds of change in the world of Calenmir.

## Further Lore
Cailega, the Queen of Winter, a captivating figure of winter and change.
### Practices and Rituals
- Winter Solstice Celebrations: Cailega's followers celebrate the Winter Solstice with bonfires, feasting, and storytelling, marking the return of the sun and the promise of renewed life.
- Ice and Snow Magic: Some practitioners of Cailega's magic specialize in weather manipulation, using spells to summon snowstorms, blizzards, and freezing winds.
- Evergreen Tree Ceremonies: Evergreen trees, sacred to Cailega, are adorned with ornaments and lights as a symbol of enduring life in the face of winter's harshness.
- Ancestor Veneration: Cailega's followers often honor their ancestors, believing that they have a connection to the spirit world and can offer guidance and protection.
- Meditation and Contemplation: Many followers practice meditation to connect with the divine and gain insight into the nature of change and transformation.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Cycle of Seasons: Life, death, and rebirth are cyclical, and winter has the power to renew the world.
- The Power of Change: Change is an inevitable and often beneficial force, embraced as a catalyst for growth and transformation.
- Respect for the Elements: Wind, snow, and ice can be both destructive and creative, and are owed respect.
- The Mystery of the Veiled One: Cailega's true nature is hidden from mortal eyes, which adds to her allure and inspires awe and reverence.
- The Strength of the Spirit: Followers are taught to be resilient and adaptable, to endure hardship, and to find strength in the face of adversity.
### Festivals
- The Festival of the Frozen Heart: Held during the peak of winter, when the land is blanketed in snow and ice. It involves ice sculpting, sledding, and other winter sports.
- The Festival of the Changing Seasons: Marks the transition from winter to spring, and is celebrated with rituals to honor the cycle of life, death, and rebirth.
- The Festival of the Veiled One: A mysterious festival held during the darkest time of the year, when Cailega's followers gather to honor her enigmatic nature.
### Clerics of Cailega
Clerics of Cailega are often rugged individuals, skilled in survival and combat. They are often found in remote, wintry regions, where they protect their communities from the harsh elements and the dangers of the wild.
### Temples of Cailega
Temples of Cailega are often carved into ice caves or built from stone and timber. They are typically located in high, mountainous regions or in dense forests. Inside, the temples are dimly lit, with intricate ice sculptures and tapestries depicting scenes of winter and change.
### Artifacts of Cailega
- The Scepter of Winter: A powerful staff that grants control over ice and snow.
- The Cloak of the Veiled One: A magical cloak that allows the wearer to become invisible and blend into the winter landscape.
- The Grimoire of the Frozen Word: A legendary tome containing spells of ice, snow, and cold.

## Relationships
- [[Manera]]: A complex relationship of contrasts. Manera is associated with growth, renewal, and the warmth of the sun; Cailega with decline, death, and the cold of winter. Winter prepares the land for the renewal of spring, so they work together, albeit indirectly, to maintain the equilibrium of the natural world, though they often vie for influence over mortal realms.
- [[Axtia]]: A tense alliance of opposites. Winter is a time of dormancy and death, but it also prepares the ground for renewal, and Axtia's measured approach to death sometimes clashes with Cailega's capricious nature.
- [[Certhys]]: A cautious alliance of opposites. Cailega's winter prepares the ground for the growth and renewal associated with Certhys' spring, and her destructive power can clear the way for new growth.
- [[Dhorer]]: A cautious alliance of opposites. Cailega's winter can provide raw materials for Dhorer's creations, but his love of order and structure can clash with her embrace of chaos and change.
- [[Ephion]]: A cautious alliance of opposites. Cailega's winter prepares the ground for the growth and renewal associated with Ephion's spring, though Ephion's love of life can clash with Cailega's embrace of death and decay.
- [[Geddal]]: A complex relationship of opposites. Geddal's strength and courage are often tested in the harsh winter landscapes that Cailega controls.
