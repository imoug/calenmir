Dhorer, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] God of Smiths and Creativity in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Talveth]]. Dhorer is a skilled craftsman deity, associated with blacksmithing and artistic endeavors. He is often depicted with a hammer and anvil, surrounded by sparks. Artisans and creators turn to him for inspiration and mastery over their crafts.

## Depictions
A strong dwarf who is always displayed tending to his forge. Shown wearing an apron and wielding his hammer Thenas' Boon.

## Holy Symbol
Dhorer's holy symbol, a hammer and anvil, perfectly encapsulates his domain as the god of Smiths and creativity. The hammer represents the strength, craftsmanship, and the shaping of raw materials into creations. The anvil, as the foundation upon which metal is forged, symbolizes stability and the transformative process that turns base materials into works of art.

This symbol serves as a beacon for artisans, blacksmiths, and creators who seek Dhorer's guidance and blessings in their creative endeavors. It also embodies the essence of ingenuity, skill, and the transformative power of mortal hands in the world of Calenmir.

## Further Lore
Dhorer, the Divine Smith, a patron of craftsmanship and order.
### Practices and Rituals
- Forge Blessings: Followers often gather around forges to pay homage to Dhorer, offering prayers and sacrifices, and smiths bless their tools and workspace before beginning a new project.
- Apprenticeship Rites: Apprenticeships are seen as sacred, and rituals are performed to mark the beginning and end of training. The first item created by a new apprentice is often offered to Dhorer as a symbol of gratitude.
- Ritual Purification: Smithing is considered a purifying process, and smiths and other craftspeople often purify themselves through ritual bathing or prayer before working.
- Festivals of Craft: Festivals are held to celebrate the skills of artisans, with competitions, exhibitions, and demonstrations.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Power of Creation: Creation is a divine act, and through hard work and skill, one can create things of beauty and utility.
- The Importance of Order: Order and structure are essential for a well-crafted life. Followers are encouraged to live disciplined lives and to strive for perfection in all their endeavors.
- The Value of Mastery: Mastery of one's craft is a noble pursuit, and followers are encouraged to continually seek to improve their skills.
- Respect for Materials: Materials, whether metal, wood, or stone, are seen as gifts from the gods and should be treated with respect.
- The Balance of Form and Function: A well-crafted object should be both beautiful and functional.
### Festivals
- The Festival of the Forge: A grand celebration of craftsmanship, featuring competitions, exhibitions, and demonstrations of skill.
- The Festival of the Hammer and Anvil: A festival honoring the tools of the trade, with rituals to bless forges and tools.
- The Festival of Innovation: A festival celebrating new ideas and inventions, encouraging creativity and ingenuity.
### Clerics of Dhorer
Clerics of Dhorer are often skilled artisans, blacksmiths, or engineers. They use their abilities to create weapons, armor, and tools, as well as to build and repair structures.
### Temples of Dhorer
Temples of Dhorer are often located near forges or mines. They are typically grand and imposing structures, made of stone and metal. Inside, the temples are filled with intricate carvings and sculptures, showcasing the skill and artistry of Dhorer's followers.
### Artifacts of Dhorer
- The Hammer of Creation: A legendary hammer capable of shaping metal and stone with ease.
- The Anvil of Eternity: An indestructible anvil that has witnessed the creation of countless artifacts.
- The Grimoire of the Smith: A tome containing knowledge of metallurgy, engineering, and other crafts.

## Relationships
- [[Manera]]: A harmonious partnership of creation and magic. Magic can inspire creativity, and skilled craftsmanship can bring magical creations to life.
- [[Axtia]]: A respectful understanding of creation and destruction. Dhorer brings objects into existence; Axtia oversees their decay and eventual destruction. Dhorer's creations, no matter how durable, eventually succumb to time and decay, and Axtia ensures the cycle continues.
- [[Cailega]]: A cautious alliance of opposites. Cailega's winter can provide raw materials for Dhorer's creations, but her embrace of chaos and change can clash with his love of order and structure.
- [[Certhys]]: A harmonious partnership of creation and inspiration. Art can inspire craftsmanship, and skilled craftsmanship can bring artistic visions to life.
- [[Ephion]]: A harmonious partnership of creation and love. Craftsmanship can inspire beauty, and beauty can inspire craftsmanship.
- [[Geddal]]: A brotherhood of craftsmen and warriors. Skilled craftsmen need physical strength to work with materials, and warriors rely on skilled craftsmen for weapons and armor; both value hard work, discipline, and the pursuit of excellence.
