Sirus, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] God of Agriculture and Waterways in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Vorneth]]. Sirus is a sturdy and dependable deity, overseeing the growth of crops and the flow of rivers. He is often depicted as a muscular figure with a plow and a jug of water. Farmers and sailors seek his favor for bountiful harvests and safe journeys.

## Depictions
A human man dressed in functional and sturdy clothes and boots. He has blond hair and green eyes. He has an enchanted scythe which he is said to have used to cut the first rivers from the land, as well as a cauldron of plenty.

## Holy Symbol
Sirus' holy symbols, either a footbridge or a sheaf of grain, represent his roles as the god of agriculture and waterways in the world of Calenmir.
- The footbridge symbolizes his connection to travel and crossing over obstacles, often depicting a sturdy bridge spanning a river or chasm. It serves as a metaphor for safe journeys and the ability to overcome challenges.
- The sheaf of grain symbolizes his domain over agriculture and fertility. It represents the bountiful harvests that he provides to his followers, highlighting his importance in sustaining communities and ensuring their well-being.
- Both symbols showcase Sirus' watchful presence over the livelihoods of those who depend on the land and waterways for their sustenance and prosperity.

## Further Lore
Sirus, God of Agriculture and Serenity, a tranquil patron of growth and balance.
### Practices and Rituals
- Harvest Festivals: Celebrations marking the bountiful harvest are central to Sirus's worship, featuring feasts, music, and offerings of the first fruits.
- Blessings for Crops and Livestock: Farmers and ranchers perform rituals to bless their fields, crops, and animals, seeking Sirus's favor for a prosperous year.
- River Offerings: Offerings are made to rivers and other bodies of water, acknowledging their importance for irrigation and sustenance.
- Meditation and Contemplation: Followers practice meditation and contemplation to connect with Sirus's serene energy and find inner peace.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Bountiful Earth: The earth is a sacred source of life, providing sustenance and nourishment.
- The Cycle of Growth and Renewal: Life, death, and rebirth are cyclical, and patience and perseverance are essential.
- The Importance of Balance: Maintaining balance in nature is crucial, and followers are encouraged to live in harmony with the environment.
- The Value of Serenity: Inner peace and tranquility are essential for a fulfilling life.
### Clerics of Sirus
Clerics of Sirus are often farmers, healers, or mediators. They use their knowledge of agriculture and nature to serve their communities and promote peace.
### Temples of Sirus
Temples of Sirus are often located near fields, rivers, or groves. They may be simple structures or elaborate complexes, reflecting Sirus's association with both nature and civilization.
### Artifacts of Sirus
- The Scythe of Plenty: A magical scythe that ensures a bountiful harvest.
- The Horn of Abundance: A horn that can summon rain and nourish the land.
- The Staff of Serenity: A staff that radiates calming energy, promoting peace and tranquility.
