Qhadar, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] Goddess of War and Strife, opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Vharnos]]. Qhadar is a fierce and determined deity, revered by warriors and strategists. She is depicted in battle armor, wielding a sword and shield. Those who seek victory in conflict and the strength to overcome adversity turn to her.

## Depictions
A human woman most commonly dressed in plate armour with a wide accompaniment of weapons but most often a spear and a long sword. She has deep orange hair and piercing dark eyes.

## Holy Symbol
Qhadar's holy symbol, two crossed spears surmounted by a raven, is a powerful representation of her domain as the goddess of war and strife.
- The crossed spears symbolize conflict, battle, and the strength of warriors. They highlight Qhadar's role in guiding those who engage in warfare, ensuring their strategies are effective and their resolve unwavering.
- The raven, often associated with intelligence and cunning, signifies Qhadar's influence over tactics and strategy on the battlefield. It also reflects her connection to the often complex and intricate nature of conflicts.
- This symbol serves as a rallying point for soldiers, strategists, and those who seek strength in times of strife in the world of Calenmir.

## Further Lore
Qhadar, Goddess of War and Victory.
### Practices and Rituals
- Offerings before Battle: Warriors often make offerings to Qhadar before battle, seeking her favor and protection. These offerings might include weapons, armor, or symbolic sacrifices.
- Rituals for the Fallen: After a battle, rituals are performed to honor the fallen, both friend and foe.
- Strategic Games and Exercises: Followers often engage in strategic games and exercises to hone their tactical skills and honor Qhadar's wisdom.
- Oaths of Service: Warriors may swear oaths of service to Qhadar, pledging their strength and loyalty to her cause.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- Honor in Combat: Qhadar's followers believe in fighting with honor and respecting their opponents. They abhor dishonorable tactics and unnecessary cruelty.
- The Glory of Victory: Victory is seen as a reward for skill, courage, and strategy.
- Respect for the Fallen: Even in the heat of battle, Qhadar's followers respect the dead and honor their sacrifices.
- The Importance of Strategy: Qhadar is seen as a goddess of strategy as well as strength. Careful planning and tactical thinking are essential for success in war.
### Clerics of Qhadar
Clerics of Qhadar are often skilled warriors and strategists. They serve as leaders and advisors, guiding their followers in matters of war and conflict.
### Temples of Qhadar
Temples of Qhadar are often located near battlefields or strategic locations. They may be simple structures or grand fortresses, reflecting Qhadar's association with both warfare and order.
### Artifacts of Qhadar
- The Spear of Victory: A legendary spear that grants its wielder unmatched skill and accuracy in battle.
- The Shield of Valor: A magical shield that protects its bearer from harm.
- The Helm of Command: A helmet that enhances the wearer's tactical abilities and inspires their troops.
