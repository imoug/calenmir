Reraura, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] God of Speech and Writing in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Xarion]]. Reraura is an eloquent and insightful deity, overseeing communication and the written word. Often depicted with a quill and a scroll, followers seek his guidance for effective expression and the preservation of knowledge.

## Depictions
A High Elven man garbed in white holding parchment with a swan's feather quill. He has grey and gold eyes, long streaming black hair and a pale complexion.

## Holy Symbol
Reraura's holy symbol, a swan's quill, perfectly encapsulates his domain as the god of speech and writing.
- The swan's quill symbolizes eloquence, communication, and the power of written words. It represents Reraura's influence over language, rhetoric, and the art of conveying thoughts and ideas through both spoken and written forms.
- This symbol serves as a reminder of Reraura's guidance in matters of expression, diplomacy, and the preservation of knowledge in the world of Calenmir. It embodies the idea of using words as tools of connection and understanding.

## Further Lore
Reraura, God of Speech, Writing, and Civilization, a divine patron of communication and order.
### Practices and Rituals
- Oaths and Agreements: Reraura's followers place great importance on oaths and agreements, believing them to be sacred bonds. Rituals are often performed to seal agreements and invoke Reraura's blessing.
- Writing and Scribing: Scribes and writers dedicate their work to Reraura, seeing it as a sacred act of preserving knowledge and communicating ideas.
- Public Speaking and Debates: Public speakers and debaters often invoke Reraura's name before addressing an audience, seeking eloquence and persuasion.
- Storytelling and Poetry: Storytellers and poets celebrate Reraura through their art, recognizing the power of language to entertain, educate, and inspire.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Power of Words: Words are powerful tools that can shape reality, influence minds, and build civilizations.
- The Importance of Communication: Clear and honest communication is essential for building understanding and fostering peace.
- The Value of Knowledge: Knowledge is sacred, and Reraura's followers are encouraged to seek wisdom and understanding.
- The Building of Civilization: Reraura is a patron of civilization, helping to establish order, laws, and social structures through communication and agreement.
### Clerics of Reraura
Clerics of Reraura are often scribes, lawyers, diplomats, or teachers. They use their knowledge of language and communication to serve their communities and promote peace.
### Temples of Reraura
Temples of Reraura are often centers of learning and scholarship, housing libraries, archives, and scriptoriums. They may also serve as places for public gatherings and debates.
### Artifacts of Reraura
- The Quill of Truth: A magical quill that writes only the truth and can reveal hidden secrets.
- The Scroll of Understanding: A scroll containing ancient wisdom on communication, diplomacy, and the power of language.
- The Swan's Feather: A feather from a swan, symbolizing eloquence, grace, and the beauty of language.
