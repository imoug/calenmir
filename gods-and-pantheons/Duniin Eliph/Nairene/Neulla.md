Neulla, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] Goddess of Sun, Light, and Fire opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Nhalys]]. Neulla is a radiant deity, associated with the warmth of the sun and the brilliance of light. Her followers look to her for inspiration, protection, and the vitality that light brings to the world.

## Depictions
She is often depicted with golden hair and fiery yellow, gold or orange eyes. 

A fun loving and excitable half elven woman garbed in yellows, oranges and red. She is portrayed as being able to summon orbs of great light or fire.

## Holy Symbol
Neulla's holy symbol, a phoenix surrounded by a holy disc, captures her essence as the goddess of Sun, light, and fire. 
- The phoenix, a mythical bird associated with rebirth and renewal, represents the cycle of day and night, as well as the rejuvenating power of the sun's warmth. 
- The holy disc symbolizes the radiant sun itself, from which Neulla draws her strength. 
- This symbol serves as a beacon of hope and inspiration for her followers, reminding them of the eternal light that guides them through both literal and metaphorical darkness in the world of Calenmir.

## Worship
A token of Neulla is commonly displayed over doorways to residences.

## Further Lore
Neulla, the Sun Keeper
### Practices and Rituals
- Sun Worship: Daily prayers and rituals are performed at sunrise and sunset to honor Neulla and the life-giving power of the sun.
- Hearth Blessings: Homes are blessed with fire and light to ensure warmth, prosperity, and protection.
- Fire Festivals: Festivals are held to celebrate the power of fire, with bonfires, torchlight processions, and fire dancing.
- Renewal Ceremonies: Rituals are performed to symbolize rebirth and renewal, often involving symbolic burning of old items and the creation of something new from the ashes.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Life-Giving Power of the Sun: The sun is seen as the source of all life and energy, and Neulla is revered as its guardian.
- The Importance of Hearth and Home: The hearth is seen as the heart of the home, a symbol of warmth, community, and family.
- The Cycle of Rebirth and Renewal: Just as the sun rises each day, life is seen as a cycle of death and rebirth, destruction and creation.
- The Cleansing Power of Fire: Fire is seen as a purifying force, capable of burning away the old and making way for the new.
### Clerics of Neulla
Clerics of Neulla often serve as guardians of temples and sacred flames. They are skilled in healing, divination, and fire magic.
### Temples of Neulla
Temples of Neulla are often built in high places, where they can be bathed in sunlight. They typically feature open-air courtyards with altars for fire rituals.
### Artifacts of Neulla
- The Solar Disc: A powerful artifact that radiates warmth and light, capable of banishing darkness and evil.
- The Phoenix Feather: A feather from a phoenix, imbued with the power of rebirth and renewal.
- The Eternal Flame: A flame that burns perpetually, symbolizing the eternal nature of the sun and Neulla's power.
