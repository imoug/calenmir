Xoemis, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] Goddess of Divination and Fate in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Vessith]]. Xoemis is a mysterious deity, holding sway over prophecy and destiny. She is often depicted with a crystal ball or a set of cards. Diviners and seekers of insight turn to her to unravel the threads of fate.

## Depictions
A tall and gaunt human woman in white tunic and black or grey robes. She is portrayed as either blind or blindfolded. She carries a club with the power of life and death and a staff with a black orb atop it encasing a white star. She has glazed purple eyes and grey hair.

## Holy Symbol
Xoemis' holy symbol, an owl, beautifully encapsulates her essence as the goddess of divination and fate.
- The owl, known for its wisdom and ability to see in the dark, symbolizes Xoemis' connection to foresight, intuition, and the hidden knowledge of the world.
- This symbol serves as a reminder of her role in guiding her followers through the mysteries of the future and helping them navigate the twists and turns of their destinies in the world of Calenmir.

## Further Lore
Xoemis, God of Divination, Fate, and Time, a mysterious and enigmatic patron of prophecy.
### Practices and Rituals
- Divination Rituals: Xoemis's followers practice various forms of divination, using crystals, tarot cards, astrology, or other tools to glimpse the future.
- Timekeeping Observances: The passage of time is carefully observed and measured, with followers often using intricate clocks, calendars, or celestial observations.
- Crystal and Gemstone Work: Working with crystals and gemstones is seen as a way to connect with Xoemis's power, as these materials are believed to hold prophetic energies.
- Meditation and Contemplation: Followers practice meditation and contemplation to quiet the mind and open themselves to prophetic visions.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Unfolding of Fate: Fate is not fixed, but a tapestry of possibilities that can be glimpsed through divination.
- The Mysteries of Time: Time is a complex and enigmatic force, with the past, present, and future intertwined.
- The Wisdom of the Owl: The owl symbolizes wisdom, intuition, and the ability to see in the dark, representing Xoemis's connection to hidden knowledge.
- The Power of Prophecy: Prophecy is a gift, but also a responsibility, as the knowledge of the future can be both a blessing and a burden.
### Clerics of Xoemis
Clerics of Xoemis are often seers, diviners, or astrologers. They use their gifts to advise others, interpret prophecies, and guide those seeking knowledge of the future.
### Temples of Xoemis
Temples of Xoemis are often located in secluded places, such as mountaintops, hidden caves, or ancient ruins. They may be filled with crystals, astronomical instruments, and other tools of divination.
### Artifacts of Xoemis
- The Crystal Ball of Visions: A magical crystal ball that allows the user to see visions of the past, present, and future.
- The Chronometer of Fate: An intricate clockwork device that can reveal the threads of fate and the turning points of time.
- The Owl's Eye Amulet: An amulet that enhances the wearer's intuition and prophetic abilities.
