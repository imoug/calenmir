Vaone, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] God of Mountains and Earth, opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Draveth]], is an enduring and solid deity, symbolizing the strength of the earth and the grandeur of mountains. His followers find solace in his stability and revere the majesty of nature's peaks.

## Depictions
He is depicted with a rocky visage and strong arms.

A stout dwarven man clothed in furs with a great battle axe and javelins. He has brown hair and pulsing green eyes. It is said that his javelins are the cause of lightning strikes and thunder is his pleasure at hitting his targets.

## Holy Symbol
Vaone's holy symbol, a mountain peak shrouded in mist, captures the essence of his role as the god of mountains and earth. The mountain peak represents the towering strength and endurance of the earth's grandest formations. The shroud of mist symbolizes the mystery and reverence associated with these majestic peaks, as well as the ever-changing nature of the natural world.
- This symbol serves as a reminder of Vaone's power to shape the landscape and provide stability to the world of Calenmir. It also embodies the spiritual connection between his followers and the awe-inspiring heights that they hold sacred.

## Worship
It is customary among the Dwarves to leave the last sip for Vaone.

## Further Lore
Vaone, God of Mountains and Earth.
### Practices and Rituals
- Mountain Pilgrimages: Followers often undertake pilgrimages to sacred mountain peaks to honor Vaone and connect with his power.
- Earth Offerings: Offerings of precious stones, minerals, or crafted items are left in caves and underground chambers as tributes to the earth.
- Beast Hunts (Respectfully): Hunts may be undertaken, not for sport, but as a ritual acknowledging the power of the earth's creatures and ensuring balance. The hunted animal is often honored.
- Stone Carving: Carving and shaping stone is seen as a sacred art, honoring Vaone's connection to the earth.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Strength of the Earth: The earth is a source of strength, stability, and enduring power.
- The Secrets of the Mountains: Mountains are revered as places of wisdom, mystery, and connection to the earth's core.
- Respect for the Othmaren: The Othmaren, as children of the earth, are held in high regard and seen as guardians of its secrets.
- The Balance of Nature: Maintaining balance in nature is crucial, and followers are encouraged to live in harmony with the earth's rhythms.
### Clerics of Vaone
Clerics of Vaone are often dwarves, miners, or earth elementals. They are skilled in communicating with the earth, shaping stone, and protecting the mountains.
### Temples of Vaone
Temples of Vaone are often located in caves, mountain grottos, or deep within the earth. They may be simple chambers or elaborate complexes carved into the rock.
### Artifacts of Vaone
- The Hammer of the Earthshaker: A legendary hammer capable of moving mountains and shaping the earth.
- The Gem of the Depths: A magical gem that grants its wearer knowledge of the earth's secrets.
- The Cloak of Stone: A magical cloak that allows the wearer to blend seamlessly into the mountains.
