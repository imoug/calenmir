Ephion, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] Goddess of Fertility and Passion in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Murneth]]. Ephion is a passionate deity, associated with both the creation of life and the intensity of emotion. She is depicted with flowing hair and vibrant colors. Couples, artists, and those seeking inspiration turn to her for vitality and creativity.

## Depictions
A human woman with long auburn hair and shining blue eyes, clothed in a flowing blue dress with long sleeves. She is commonly portrayed dancing with a rapier.

## Holy Symbol
Ephion's holy symbol is an intertwined vine with blooming flowers. The intertwined vine represents the connection between life and growth, while the blooming flowers symbolize the passion and vitality that Ephion governs over. This symbol beautifully captures her roles in nurturing life, inspiring passion, and fostering creative expression in the world of Calenmir.

## Further Lore
Ephion, the Goddess of Fertility and Passion, a divine muse of love and life.
### Practices and Rituals
- Spring Festivals: Ephion's followers celebrate the arrival of spring with festivals of love, fertility, and renewal.
- Love Rituals: Couples often perform rituals to strengthen their bonds and seek blessings for fertility.
- Poetic Contests: Poets and bards compete in contests to honor Ephion's love of language and the written word, and often gather to share their work and inspire one another.
- Nature Worship: Ephion's followers often connect with nature, seeing it as a manifestation of her divine power.
- Healing Rituals: Ephion's followers often use music and poetry to heal emotional wounds and promote well-being.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Power of Love: Love is seen as a powerful force that can heal, inspire, and transform.
- The Joy of Life: Ephion's followers embrace the joys of life, including love, beauty, and creativity.
- The Importance of Family: Family is considered sacred, and followers are encouraged to nurture strong bonds with their loved ones.
- The Beauty of Language: Language is seen as a powerful tool for expressing love, passion, and creativity.
- The Harmony of Nature: Humans are connected to the natural world, and followers strive to live in harmony with nature, treating all living beings with kindness and compassion.
### Festivals
- The Festival of Love and Beauty: A celebration of love, beauty, and creativity, featuring music, dance, poetry, and art.
- The Festival of Fertility: A festival honoring the cycle of life and death, with rituals to promote fertility and abundance.
- The Festival of the Muse: A festival dedicated to the arts, with competitions in poetry, music, and storytelling.
### Clerics of Ephion
Clerics of Ephion are often bards, poets, or healers. They use their skills to inspire and uplift others, and to promote love, peace, and harmony.
### Temples of Ephion
Temples of Ephion are often located in beautiful natural settings, such as forests or meadows. They are typically adorned with flowers, music, and art.
### Artifacts of Ephion
- The Lyre of the Muse: A magical lyre that can inspire creativity and evoke powerful emotions.
- The Amulet of Love: A charm that can attract love and passion.
- The Scroll of Infinite Poetry: A legendary scroll containing countless poems and songs.

## Relationships
- [[Manera]]: A harmonious partnership of creation and magic. Magic can inspire love and beauty, and love and beauty can inspire magical creations.
- [[Axtia]]: A respectful understanding of life and death. Ephion brings new life into the world; Axtia oversees the transition from life to death. They maintain a respectful distance.
- [[Cailega]]: A cautious alliance of opposites. Cailega's winter prepares the ground for the growth and renewal associated with Ephion's spring, though Ephion's love of life can clash with Cailega's embrace of death and decay.
- [[Certhys]]: A harmonious partnership of creativity and love. Art can inspire love, and love can inspire art.
- [[Dhorer]]: A harmonious partnership of creation and love. Craftsmanship can inspire beauty, and beauty can inspire craftsmanship.
- [[Geddal]]: A respectful alliance of contrasting energies. Geddal's emphasis on action and physical prowess may clash with Ephion's more contemplative and emotional nature, but both strength and love are necessary for a fulfilling life.
