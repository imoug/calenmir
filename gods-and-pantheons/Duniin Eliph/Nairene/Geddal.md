## Depictions
A strong human man with long brown hair and twinkling blue eyes. Garbed in sturdy and functional clothes with a sheathed sword and a backpack. He is often depicted with bulging muscles and a fearless expression.

## Holy Symbol
Geddal's holy symbol, a silver hand, aptly encapsulates his domain as the god of strength and adventure.
- The silver hand symbolizes power, courage, and the ability to overcome challenges. It represents Geddal's role in empowering his followers with the physical and mental strength needed to embark on daring adventures and face adversity.
- This symbol serves as a beacon for those who seek bravery, resilience, and the thrill of venturing into the unknown in the world of Calenmir. It embodies the idea of pushing one's limits and embracing the spirit of adventure under Geddal's watchful gaze.

## Further Lore
Geddal, the God of Strength and Adventure.
### Practices and Rituals
- Trials of Strength: Followers often engage in physical trials to test their strength and endurance.
- Combat Training: Warriors and mercenaries train rigorously, seeking to hone their skills and honor Geddal.
- Pilgrimages: Many followers embark on pilgrimages to sacred sites associated with Geddal, seeking blessings and challenges.
- Oaths of Loyalty: Followers often swear oaths of loyalty to Geddal, promising to uphold his ideals of courage and honor.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Power of Strength: Physical strength is valued, but inner strength is considered even more important.
- The Spirit of Adventure: A life without adventure is a life wasted. Followers are encouraged to seek out challenges and explore the unknown.
- Honor and Loyalty: Honor is paramount, and followers are expected to keep their word and protect those who cannot protect themselves.
- The Thrill of Battle: Battle is seen as a test of strength and courage, and followers are encouraged to face their fears and embrace the thrill of combat.
- The Importance of Discipline: Discipline is essential for both physical and mental strength. Followers are encouraged to train rigorously and to live a disciplined life, using their strength and skills for the betterment of others.
### Festivals
- The Festival of the Strong: A celebration of physical prowess, featuring competitions in wrestling, archery, and other martial arts.
- The Festival of the Adventurer: A festival honoring the spirit of exploration, with storytelling, music, and performances by traveling minstrels.
- The Festival of the Warrior: A solemn festival dedicated to those who have fallen in battle, with rituals to honor their sacrifice.
### Clerics of Geddal
Clerics of Geddal are often warriors, knights, or adventurers. They use their strength and skills to protect the innocent and to uphold justice.
### Temples of Geddal
Temples of Geddal are often located in fortified castles or mountain strongholds. They are typically austere and functional, with training grounds and armories.
### Artifacts of Geddal
- The Sword of the Valiant: A legendary sword that grants its wielder immense strength and power.
- The Armor of the Invulnerable: A suit of armor that can withstand any attack.
- The Grimoire of the Warrior: A tome containing knowledge of combat, strategy, and the art of war.

## Relationships
- [[Manera]]: A respectful understanding of opposing forces. Geddal embodies the physical and material realm; Manera the spiritual and magical. They recognize the importance of balance between strength and wisdom, action and contemplation.
- [[Axtia]]: A respectful understanding of life and death. Geddal embodies the life force and the will to survive; Axtia oversees the transition from life to death. Geddal's warriors, however strong, eventually succumb to death, and Axtia ensures the cycle continues.
- [[Cailega]]: A complex relationship of opposites. Geddal's strength and courage are often tested in the harsh winter landscapes that Cailega controls, and her destructive power can clear the way for new growth.
- [[Certhys]]: A cautious alliance of contrasting energies. Geddal's emphasis on action and physical prowess may clash with Certhys' more contemplative and creative nature, but both recognize that strength and creativity are necessary for survival and prosperity.
- [[Dhorer]]: A brotherhood of craftsmen and warriors. Skilled craftsmen need physical strength to work with materials, and warriors rely on skilled craftsmen to provide them with weapons and armor. Both deities value hard work, discipline, and the pursuit of excellence.
- [[Ephion]]: A respectful alliance of contrasting energies. Geddal's emphasis on action may clash with Ephion's more contemplative and emotional nature, but both recognize that strength and love are necessary for a fulfilling life.
