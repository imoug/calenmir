Iaras, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] God of Medicine and Healing in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Vereth]]. Iaras is a benevolent deity who watches over healers and physicians. Often depicted with a staff and a soothing demeanor, he offers solace and aid to those suffering from ailments. His followers believe in the power of compassion and herbal remedies to mend both body and spirit, and with domains of Life and Peace his care extends beyond physical healing to encompass mental and spiritual well-being.

## Depictions
A sprightly elderly human with a simple tunic and red cloak. He carries a small sack, enchanted to be a bag of holding.

## Holy Symbol
Iaras' holy symbol, crossed oak and mistletoe branches, embodies his role as the benevolent deity of medicine and healing. The oak branch represents strength, endurance, and the vitality of life. It signifies the natural world's capacity for renewal and growth. The mistletoe, a plant often associated with healing and protection, symbolizes Iaras' ability to bring relief and comfort to those in need. Together, these intertwined branches serve as a reminder of the balance between strength and tenderness, and they inspire his followers to seek healing and compassion in the world of Calenmir.

## Further Lore
Iaras, the Divine Healer, a guardian of life and peace.
### Practices and Rituals
- Meditation and Mindfulness: Practices that promote inner peace and tranquility are central to Iaras' worship.
- Community Healing: Rituals are performed to heal rifts between individuals and communities, fostering harmony and understanding.
- Nature Worship: Connecting with nature is seen as a way to restore balance and vitality.
- Ancestral Healing: Honoring ancestors, especially those who were healers, is a common practice.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Interconnectedness of All Life: All living beings are seen as part of a larger cosmic web, and harming one harms all.
- The Power of Peace: Peace is the foundation of a healthy and prosperous society.
- The Importance of Compassion: Compassion and empathy are essential for healing, both physical and emotional.
- The Balance of Mind, Body, and Spirit: A holistic approach to health, emphasizing the importance of physical, mental, and spiritual well-being.
### Clerics of Iaras
Clerics of Iaras often serve as healers, counselors, and peacemakers. They use their knowledge of medicine, psychology, and spiritual practices to help others.
### Temples of Iaras
Temples of Iaras are often serene and peaceful places, often located in natural settings. They may include gardens, healing pools, and meditation rooms.
### Artifacts of Iaras
- The Staff of Life: A magical staff that can heal wounds and restore vitality.
- The Elixir of Peace: A potion that can calm the mind and soothe the soul.
- The Scroll of Serenity: A scroll containing ancient wisdom on meditation, mindfulness, and inner peace.
