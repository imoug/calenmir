Manera, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] Goddess of Magic in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Selvane]]. Manera is a mysterious and enigmatic deity, known for her mastery over the arcane arts. She is often depicted as a veiled figure, shrouded in swirling mists, her hands radiating with magical energy. Followers seek her guidance to unlock the secrets of the cosmos and harness the power of spells.

## Depictions
A Moon Elven woman with flowing purple and red robes. She holds a magical staff, a diadem with a silver moon and a book chained to her side. She has a chariot called Moonchaser. She has purple eyes and medium length auburn hair.

## Holy Symbol
Manera's holy symbol, a setting sun crowned by seven purple stars between five golden rays, represents the mystical balance between the fading light of day and the emergence of the night sky's stars. The sun signifies her connection to magic's illuminating power, while the stars symbolize the secrets of the cosmos that her followers seek to unlock. The golden rays embody the radiance of her guidance, and the purple stars highlight her affinity with the arcane and otherworldly. This symbol serves as a beacon for those who look to Manera for wisdom and mastery over magic in the world of Calenmir.

## Further Lore
Manera, the High Queen of Magic. Her followers are a diverse group including wizards, bards, navigators, sea captains, sages, astronomers, and spellcasters.
### Practices and Rituals
- Stargazing and Celestial Mapping: Followers often spend nights observing the stars, charting their movements, and seeking signs and portents.
- Moonlit Ceremonies: Many rituals are held during the full moon, as it is seen as a time of heightened magical power. These ceremonies often involve offerings, chants, and the casting of spells.
- Arcane Study and Research: Scholars and mages dedicate their lives to studying the arcane arts, seeking knowledge and understanding of the universe.
- Meditation and Contemplation: Many followers practice meditation to connect with the divine and gain insight into the nature of reality.
- Rites by Calling: Wizards perform complex rituals to summon and control magical energies; bards use music and poetry to honor Manera; navigators and sea captains offer sacrifices for safe passage and favorable winds; sages and astronomers study the celestial bodies to predict future events.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Interconnectedness of All Things: All things in the universe are interconnected, and magic is the force that binds them together.
- The Power of Knowledge: Knowledge is revered as a sacred and powerful tool. By studying the world and its mysteries, one can gain insight into the nature of reality and harness its power.
- Balance and Harmony: Followers strive to maintain balance in all things, recognizing that excess can lead to chaos and destruction.
- Respect for Nature: Nature is seen as a manifestation of Manera's power, and it is important to treat it with respect and reverence.
- The Cycle of Life and Death: Life and death are part of a natural cycle, and magic can be used to heal and renew, as well as to destroy.
- The Divine Spark Within: Each individual possesses a divine spark, a connection to the cosmic magic that flows through all things. By tapping into this inner power, one can achieve great things, but great power comes with responsibility, and magic must be used ethically and for the benefit of others.
### Festivals
- The Festival of the Seven Stars: A grand celebration held annually to honor the seven stars that crown Manera's symbol, involving elaborate processions, magical displays, and feasts.
- The Equinox Festival: Marks the balance between day and night, celebrated with rituals to ensure harmony in the natural world.
- The Solstice Festival: Honors the turning of the seasons, with celebrations focused on the power of the sun and moon.
### Clerics of Manera
Clerics of Manera are often versatile spellcasters, skilled in both offensive and defensive magic. They are also adept at divination and illusion. Their roles in society vary widely, from court wizards and astrologers to wandering sages and hermit scholars.
### Temples of Manera
Temples of Manera are often grand structures with soaring spires and intricate carvings. They are typically located in secluded places, such as mountaintops or hidden valleys. Inside, the temples are filled with bookshelves, astronomical instruments, and magical artifacts.
### Artifacts of Manera
- The Moonstone of Manera: A powerful artifact that grants its wielder control over lunar magic.
- The Starweaver's Staff: A staff that allows the user to manipulate the stars and constellations.
- The Grimoire of the High Queen: A legendary tome containing forbidden knowledge and powerful spells.

## Relationships
- [[Axtia]]: A complex relationship of balance. Manera, the goddess of creation and growth, brings life into the world; Axtia ensures that the cycle continues. They share a deep respect for each other's power and ultimately recognize the importance of their partnership in maintaining cosmic order.
- [[Cailega]]: A complex relationship of contrasts. Manera is associated with growth, renewal, and the warmth of the sun; Cailega with decline, death, and the cold of winter. They recognize the interdependence of their domains and often work together, albeit indirectly, to maintain the equilibrium of the natural world, though they often vie for influence over mortal realms.
- [[Certhys]]: A harmonious partnership of creativity and magic. Magic can inspire art, and art can inspire magic.
- [[Dhorer]]: A harmonious partnership of creation and magic. Magic can inspire creativity, and skilled craftsmanship can bring magical creations to life.
- [[Ephion]]: A harmonious partnership of creation and magic. Magic can inspire love and beauty, and love and beauty can inspire magical creations.
- [[Geddal]]: A respectful understanding of opposing forces. Geddal embodies the physical and material realm; Manera the spiritual and magical. They recognize the importance of balance between strength and wisdom, action and contemplation.
