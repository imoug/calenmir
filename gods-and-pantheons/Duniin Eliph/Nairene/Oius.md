Oius, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] God of the Seas in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Ouroth]]. Oius is a tempestuous deity, embodying the power and mystery of the oceans. He is depicted with a trident and waves crashing around him. Sailors and fishermen pay homage to him for calm seas and abundant catches.

## Depictions
A human man with a large brimmed hat and half quarter trousers. He has black hair, turquoise eyes, a sabre and a trident.

## Holy Symbol
Oius' holy symbol, white horses, beautifully encapsulates his domain as the god of the seas. The symbol of white horses represents the powerful, majestic, and untamed waves that surge across the oceans. This image serves as a reminder of Oius' might and influence over the waters, as well as his role in providing safe journeys and abundance to sailors and those who rely on the sea for their livelihoods in the world of Calenmir.

## Further Lore
Oius, God of the Oceans, a majestic ruler of the seas.
### Practices and Rituals
- Offerings to the Sea: Sailors and coastal communities make offerings to Oius to ensure safe passage and bountiful catches. These offerings often include precious items thrown into the sea, or small boats set adrift.
- Shipbuilding Ceremonies: Shipwrights perform rituals to bless new ships and ensure their seaworthiness. These ceremonies might involve symbolic sacrifices or the carving of protective runes.
- Tidal Observances: Coastal communities observe the tides closely, understanding their connection to Oius's power. Special rituals may be performed during high or low tides.
- Sea Creature Festivals: Festivals celebrate the diverse creatures of the sea, with storytelling, music, and dances that mimic the movements of marine life.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Power of the Ocean: The ocean is a powerful and unpredictable force, deserving of respect and reverence.
- The Interconnectedness of Life: All sea creatures are considered sacred, and harming them is an offense against Oius.
- The Cycle of Tides: The ebb and flow of the tides is a metaphor for the cycles of life, death, and rebirth.
- The Mystery of the Deep: The depths of the ocean are a realm of mystery and wonder, full of untold secrets and hidden dangers.
### Clerics of Oius
Clerics of Oius are often sailors, fishermen, or coastal dwellers. They are skilled in navigating, predicting weather, and communicating with sea creatures.
### Temples of Oius
Temples of Oius are often located near the coast, overlooking the sea. They may be built on cliffs, islands, or even on floating platforms.
### Artifacts of Oius
- The Trident of the Depths: A powerful trident that grants its wielder control over the tides and sea creatures.
- The Shell of Whispers: A magical shell that allows the user to hear the voices of the ocean.
- The Chart of the Currents: A legendary chart that reveals the hidden currents and pathways of the sea.
