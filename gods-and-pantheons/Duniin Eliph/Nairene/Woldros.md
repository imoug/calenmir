Woldros, the [[Nairene|Nairean]] God of Luck and Wit in opposition to [[Daeherut]] [[Nerith]]. Woldros is a playful and cunning deity, embodying both luck and cleverness. He is often depicted with a mischievous grin and a pair of dice. Gamblers and tricksters seek his favor for good fortune and wily solutions.

## Depictions
A rotund human man dressed in luxurious clothes of bright contrasting colours, normally gold, purple or orange. He is known to carry either scales, a harp or a baton. He is known to hum away to himself, and anyone in earshot will then also start singing, dancing or humming the same.

## Holy Symbol
Woldros' holy symbol, a harp, is a fitting representation of his role as the god of luck and wit.
- The harp symbolizes harmony, rhythm, and the interplay of elements. It represents Woldros' influence in bringing balance to the ebb and flow of luck, as well as his domain over cleverness and wittiness.
- This symbol serves as a reminder of Woldros' hand in shaping fortune and encouraging clever solutions in the world of Calenmir. It embodies the idea of embracing the ups and downs of life with a playful and ingenious spirit.

## Further Lore
Woldros, God of Luck, Music, and Wit, a charming and unpredictable patron of fortune.
### Practices and Rituals
- Gambling and Games of Chance: Followers often engage in games of chance, believing that Woldros influences the outcome.
- Musical Performances: Music is central to Woldros's worship, with followers playing instruments, singing, and dancing in his honor.
- Improvisation and Storytelling: Spontaneous performances, witty banter, and clever storytelling are ways to honor Woldros's playful nature.
- Offerings of Shiny Objects: Shiny coins, jewels, or other trinkets are often offered to Woldros, symbolizing the unpredictable nature of luck.
### Dogma and Beliefs
- The Wheel of Fortune: Luck is a constantly turning wheel, bringing both good fortune and misfortune.
- The Power of Wit: Cleverness, humor, and quick thinking are valued traits, seen as gifts from Woldros.
- The Joy of Music: Music is a way to express joy, celebrate life, and connect with the divine.
- Embracing Uncertainty: Followers embrace the unpredictable nature of life, understanding that change and chance are part of the journey.
### Clerics of Woldros
Clerics of Woldros are often musicians, performers, or rogues. They use their talents to entertain, inspire, and sometimes to outwit their opponents.
### Temples of Woldros
Temples of Woldros are often lively places, filled with music, laughter, and the sounds of games. They may be located in taverns, theaters, or hidden groves.
### Artifacts of Woldros
- The Harp of Fortune: A magical harp that can influence luck and bring good fortune to its player.
- The Coin of Chance: A coin that always lands on the desired side, but only once.
- The Mask of Wit: A mask that enhances the wearer's cleverness and humor.
