**Type:** Religion  
**Alternative names:** the Tidebourne, the Salt-Kissed  
**Adherents:** the [[Kingdom of Thalassa|Thalassans]] and those who live by the coast or make their livings on the seas  
**Divine Domains for clerics:** _Death, Life, Knowledge, Nature, Tempest_  

A religion worshipping the sea itself, popular among the [[Kingdom of Thalassa|Thalassans]] and those who live by the coast or make their livings on the seas. Some argue that this is a mere aspect of [[Oius]], [[Ouroth]] or both combined. Adherents believe the sea is a living entity that holds great power and knowledge: the **Abyssal Maw**, a being of immense age and fathomless depth.

## Core Beliefs
- **The Sea as a Living Entity:** The cult views the ocean not as a mere body of water, but as a sentient, powerful entity, the Abyssal Maw, a being of immense age and fathomless depth.
- **The Cycle of Drowning and Rebirth:** They believe that true devotion to the Abyssal Maw requires a symbolic drowning, a surrender to the depths, followed by a rebirth into the Maw's embrace. This "drowning" can take many forms, from ritual immersion to near-death experiences at sea.
- **The Power of the Tides:** The tides are seen as the breath of the Abyssal Maw, its moods and desires made manifest. High tides represent its hunger and power, while low tides are moments of quiet contemplation.
- **The Secrets of the Deep:** The depths of the ocean hold ancient secrets and forbidden knowledge, which the cult seeks to uncover through rituals, visions, and communion with the Maw.
- **The Drowned Ones:** Those who have truly surrendered to the Maw are believed to become "Drowned Ones," beings touched by the sea's power, capable of wielding its magic and understanding its whispers.

## Practices and Rituals
- **Ritual Drownings:** Initiates undergo ritual immersions, often in cold, turbulent waters, to symbolize their surrender to the Maw.
- **Tide Readings:** Priests and priestesses study the tides, interpreting their patterns and movements as messages from the Abyssal Maw.
- **Deep-Sea Vigils:** Cult members spend nights on the open sea, gazing into the depths, seeking visions and communion with the Maw.
- **Offerings to the Maw:** Offerings of precious objects, sea creatures, and even human sacrifices are made to appease the Maw and gain its favor.
- **The Calling of the Drowned Ones:** Rituals are performed to summon and commune with the Drowned Ones, seeking their wisdom and guidance.
- **Whisper Stones:** Carved stones that are placed in the sea, and then retrieved. It is believed they hold whispers of the Abyssal Maw.

## Hierarchy and Structure
- **The High Tide Speaker:** The leader of the cult, believed to be the voice of the Abyssal Maw.
- **Tide Readers:** Priests and priestesses who interpret the tides and perform rituals.
- **Drowned Ones:** Individuals who have undergone the true drowning and are considered conduits of the Maw's power.
- **The Brethren of the Depths:** The general membership of the cult, devoted to the Abyssal Maw.

## Appearance and Symbolism
- **Seaweed and Coral Adornments:** Cult members often wear adornments made from seaweed, coral, and shells.
- **Tidal Tattoos:** Tattoos depicting waves, sea creatures, and the symbols of the Abyssal Maw.
- **Drowned Garments:** Priests and priestesses wear garments soaked in seawater, often with a greenish, decaying hue.
- **The Maw's Eye:** A symbol representing the Abyssal Maw, often depicted as a swirling vortex or a single, all-seeing eye.

## Themes and Atmosphere
- **Fear and Awe:** The cult inspires both fear and awe, reflecting the power and unpredictability of the sea.
- **Mystery and Secrecy:** The cult's rituals and beliefs are shrouded in mystery, adding to their unsettling aura.
- **Devotion and Fanaticism:** Cult members are fiercely devoted to the Abyssal Maw, often to the point of fanaticism.
- **Decay and Transformation:** The cult embraces the cycle of decay and rebirth, seeing it as a necessary part of the Maw's embrace.
