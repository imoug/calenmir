**Type:** Religion  
**Alternative names:** the Chant of Light  
**Primary deity:** [[She Who Sings]]  
**Organisation:** the [[Chantry]]  

A monotheistic religion which was founded by a Drow who led a rebellion against the rule of Lolth and her matriarchs. She led her people to the surface and founded the city of [[Choral Dominion of Helegos|Helegos]].

## Organisation
- Anthem is led by the [[Chantry]] and its [[High Chanter]] who is guided by [[The Choir|the Choir]].
- The Chantry is subdivided into a number of "provinces" which mirror the political make-up of human lands immediately post conquest, aside from a few small fixes. This has led to some strangeness where an area's province does not adhere to its national affiliation.
- Openly proselytising in the north western and western lands of [[Calenmir]].

## Beliefs
- Individuals that are touched by [[The Divine Song|the Divine Song]], or the Chant of Light, are known as Chanters. They can be of any race, class, creed or nationality, therefore spurring Anthem to be diverse and accepting of all races lest they inadvertently harm a potential [[Chanter]].
- Due to the monotheistic nature of this religion a cleric of Anthem can fulfil any domain.

## Relations
- Enemies of Lolth and have disdain for all other pantheons.
