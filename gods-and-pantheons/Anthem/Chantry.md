**Founded by:** the [[Augur]]  

Human religious organisation built in worship to Meloda, led by a council of Matriarchs based in various human cities, nominally led by the [[High Chanter]].

## History
It is said the Chantry was founded by a Drow who first heard the [[Chant of Light]] and who led a rebellion against the rule of [Lolth](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lolth) and her matriarchs. She is known as the [[Augur]] in the Chantry.

The religion originated in the continent [[Palarinor]] which has spread to the main land of [[Calenmir]].

## Beliefs
They practice the "[[Anthem]]" and revere the mother goddess known to them as the Chanter or Singer, which they believe led to the creation of the universe. They reject the [[Duniin Eliph]] who are widely worshipped in various forms throughout Calenmir and [[Iqela]].

## Structure
- The High Chanter is only known by their title.
- They are given a posthumous name.
- The Chantry is subdivided into a number of "provinces" which mirror the political make-up of human lands immediately post conquest, aside from a few small fixes. This has led to some strangeness where an area's province does not adhere to its national affiliation.
