Once the creator goddess's favorite, now consumed by the [[Void]] and the great adversary of [[Anthem]] cosmology.

## True Nature
- The Hollow King wasn't **always a monster**. He was the **creator goddess's favorite**, tasked with **guarding the Void's prison**. But when she **sealed herself**, she **abandoned him**, and the **Void consumed him** as punishment.
- He's **not a villain**, he's a **wounded animal**. He **hates the creator** for leaving him, but he **can't stop consuming** because that's all the Void left him.
- When he **speaks**, his voice **erases words** from the listener's mind. His **crown** isn't a symbol of power, it's a **muzzle**, and if it's removed, he **screams so loud it unwrites sound**.
