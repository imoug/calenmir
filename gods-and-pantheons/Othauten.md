**Type:** Pantheon  
**Alternative names:**  
**Headed by:** Lolth, the Queen of Spiders  

A colloquial surface term for the prominent gods worshipped in the [[Othmaren]], or the underworld of [[Calenmir]]; which for any adherent probably wouldn't be connected at all. This pantheon is headed by Lolth the Queen of Spiders who is the most powerful deity worshipped in the Othmaren.

>See: [[Othmaren]], [[Anthem]], [[Duniin Eliph]]
