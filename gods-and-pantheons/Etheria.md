An astral goddess from far beyond [[Calenmir]], she embodies the [[Ethersea]]. She is known by many other names including *Mirieth* to the [[Arlithrim]].

## Worship
Ancient sailors of the Ethersea were said to offer prayers to her for protection across her domain.

## History
It is said that Etheria had a hand in the [[Cataclysm]] which destroyed [[Arlith]]. She is linked with [[Etherstorm|Etherstorms]] and [[The Black Tide]].
