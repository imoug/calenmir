**Type:** Pantheon  
**Alternative names:**   
**Pantheon of:** the [[Suthlonir]]  

A pantheon worshipped by the [[Suthlonir]], a group of humans primarily located in the northern continent of [[Palarinor]] and north-western parts of [[Calenmir]].

## Roster
- [[Wodan]]; wisdom, poetry, death, divination and magic (knowledge, arcana & war domains)
- [[Donar]]; thunder, lightning, strength and protection (tempest & war domains)
- [[Frija]]; love, beauty, fertility and the sun (life & light domains)
- [[Tiwaz]]; law, justice and the forge (light, order & forge domains)
- [[Frikka]]; marriage, motherhood, prophecy and the home (life & peace domains)
- [[Waldemar]]; loyalty and watchfulness (light & order domains)
- [[Loder]]; fire, chaos and trickery (trickery & death domains)
- [[Idunna]]; moon and death (twilight & grave domains)
- [[Grimwald]]; poetry, music and storytelling (life & light domains)
- [[Fjörnir]]; sea, wind, fishing and wealth (nature & tempest domains)
- [[Ingui]]; fertility, agriculture, peace and prosperity (life & nature domains)
