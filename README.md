# Calenmir

Welcome to Calenmir, a world steeped in ancient magic, where the veil between the mundane and the mystical runs thin. Long ago, a great cataclysm, its true cause shrouded in myth and legend, reshaped the land and left the gods in retreat, their influence dwindling with each passing century. From vast forests and soaring mountain ranges to desolate wastelands scarred by past conflicts, the continent spans landscapes as varied as the peoples who call them home. In the wake of the cataclysm, mortal empires, kingdoms, and states have risen and fallen, some seeking lost ancient knowledge, others reunion with the gods, and others still simply exploiting the world for their own ends. Its many cultures, from nomadic tribes roaming the plains to agrarian valley communities and seafaring folk who brave the treacherous coasts, live alongside the wild and unpredictable magic of the Sarifana, the Gift created and maintained by the goddess Manera.
